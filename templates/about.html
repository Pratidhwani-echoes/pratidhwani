The constellation

About this exhibit

An echo of missions that never flew.

Pratidhwani is an interactive exhibit about eight spacecraft and programs that were built, tested, cancelled, or lost before they could fulfill their intended missions. Explore their stories through the voices of the hardware.

The first-person narration is a creative retelling. Dates, technical details, and historical context are drawn from public mission records and technical reports; the narration is not an official NASA publication.
