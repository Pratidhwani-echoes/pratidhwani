About this exhibit

Pratidhwani - an echo of abandoned missions.

Pratidhwani is an interactive exhibit about eight spacecraft and programs that have contributed to our understanding of the Universe of which we are a part. This website is an attempt to preserve the memory of these missions.

It is aimed at teaching school-age space enthusiasts and curious minds of all ages about human ingenuity and innovation. Thus, a gamified approach involving the principle of "active learning" is emphasized by following up the reading with a quiz and questions to ponder. To ensure ease of use and accessibility, a dictionary API is utilised to provide definitions within the website itself, eliminating the need for users to navigate away from the exhibit.

If you find any errors, omissions or inaccuracies, please contact the author at Saanvishree@proton.me.

A first-person narration is employed as a creative tool. Dates, technical details, and historical context are drawn from public mission records by NASA; the narration is not an official NASA publication.
